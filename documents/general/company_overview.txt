COMPANY OVERVIEW — BJNEXUS AI
Hyderabad, India | 2026

WHO WE ARE
BJNEXUS AI is an AI automation company specializing in Enterprise RAG Systems, Voice Agents, and intelligent workflow automation. Founded by Karuna Alekya K., the company serves clients across India, North America, and Europe.

OUR MISSION
To make enterprise AI reliable, explainable, and production-ready — systems that know what they don't know and say so, instead of guessing.

CORE SERVICES
1. Enterprise RAG Systems
   Custom document intelligence systems with hybrid retrieval, reranking, and hallucination prevention. Built on pgvector, Cohere, and open-source LLMs.

2. Voice Agents
   AI-powered voice agents for lead qualification, appointment booking, and customer support. Live demo: +1 517 923 2840.

3. AI Workflow Automation
   End-to-end automation using Make.com, N8N, GoHighLevel, Apollo, and Smartlead for lead generation and CRM workflows.

4. Consulting & Training
   AI strategy consulting and team training for enterprises adopting AI.

TECHNOLOGY STACK
- LLMs: Groq (llama-3.3-70b), OpenAI GPT-4o, Ollama (local)
- Vector DB: PostgreSQL + pgvector
- Reranking: Cohere Rerank v3
- Embeddings: sentence-transformers, OpenAI text-embedding-3-large
- Backend: FastAPI, SQLAlchemy
- Frontend: Streamlit, React
- Deployment: Railway, Docker
