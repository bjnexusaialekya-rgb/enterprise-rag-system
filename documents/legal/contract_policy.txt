CONTRACT POLICY — BJNEXUS AI
Effective January 2026

PURPOSE
This policy governs how BJNEXUS AI enters into, manages, and closes contracts with clients, vendors, and partners.

CONTRACT TYPES
1. Fixed Price Contracts — Defined scope, fixed deliverables, fixed price. Used for RAG builds, automation projects.
2. Time & Material Contracts — Hourly or daily rate. Used for consulting and support engagements.
3. Retainer Contracts — Monthly fixed fee for ongoing support and maintenance.

CONTRACT APPROVAL
All contracts above USD 1,000 require approval from the Managing Director. Contracts above USD 10,000 require legal review before signing.

PAYMENT TERMS
Standard payment terms are 50% upfront and 50% on delivery for fixed price contracts. Retainers are invoiced monthly in advance.

MILESTONE PAYMENTS
For projects exceeding USD 2,000, milestone-based payments are preferred: 30% on signing, 40% at mid-point delivery, 30% on final delivery.

SCOPE CHANGES
Any change in scope must be documented via a Change Request Form. Additional cost and timeline impact must be agreed in writing before work begins.

TERMINATION
Either party may terminate a contract with 14 days written notice. Work completed up to termination date will be invoiced at the agreed rate.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All custom code and deliverables become the property of the client upon full payment. BJNEXUS AI retains the right to use anonymized project details for portfolio purposes.

DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Disputes shall first be attempted through mediation. If unresolved within 30 days, disputes shall be referred to arbitration under Indian Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996.
