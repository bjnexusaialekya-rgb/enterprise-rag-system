LEAVE POLICY — BJNEXUS AI
Effective January 2026

ANNUAL LEAVE
Every employee is entitled to 21 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be applied for at least 3 days in advance except in emergencies. Unused leave up to 10 days may be carried forward to the next year.

SICK LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

CASUAL LEAVE
5 days of casual leave are provided per year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
BJNEXUS AI observes 12 public holidays per year as declared by the Government of Telangana.

MATERNITY LEAVE
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave as per the Maternity Benefit Act 1961.

PATERNITY LEAVE
Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave within 3 months of the child's birth.

LEAVE WITHOUT PAY
Leave without pay may be granted at the discretion of the management for a maximum of 30 days per year.

LEAVE APPLICATION PROCESS
All leave requests must be submitted via the HR portal. Approval is at the discretion of the department head. Emergency leave must be communicated within 2 hours of absence.
