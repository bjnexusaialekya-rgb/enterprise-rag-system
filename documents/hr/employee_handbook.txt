EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK — BJNEXUS AI
Version 1.0 | Effective January 2026

WELCOME
Welcome to BJNEXUS AI. This handbook outlines company policies, expectations, and benefits for all employees.

WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Monday to Friday. Flexible work arrangements may be approved by department heads on a case-by-case basis.

CODE OF CONDUCT
All employees are expected to maintain professional conduct at all times. This includes respectful communication, confidentiality of client information, and adherence to company values.

PROBATION PERIOD
All new employees undergo a 90-day probation period. Performance is reviewed at 30, 60, and 90 days. Confirmation of employment is subject to satisfactory performance.

PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Annual performance reviews are conducted every December. Mid-year check-ins are held in June. Ratings are: Exceptional, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement.

BENEFITS
- Health insurance covered 100% for employee
- 21 days paid annual leave
- 10 days sick leave
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks
- Paternity leave: 2 weeks
- Internet allowance: INR 1,000/month
- Learning budget: INR 25,000/year

GRIEVANCE POLICY
Employees may raise grievances with their direct manager or HR. All grievances are acknowledged within 2 business days and resolved within 15 business days.
